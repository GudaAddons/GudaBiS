# GudaBiS

Best-in-slot gear by phase, docked beside your Character frame, for every World of Warcraft flavor.

- A BiS paperdoll next to the Character frame, with a phase dropdown and your own named lists
- Checkmarks for BiS items you already own (equipped, bags, bank, other characters)
- Recommended enchants and gems per slot, with a warning when yours are missing
- "Your BiS dropped" and "Rolling now" alerts for group loot, master loot, Gargul and RCLootCouncil
- Optional auto-roll (off by default) and a loot history of your last raids and dungeons

Guide data is based on Wowhead's BiS guides.

Slash command: `/gbis`
